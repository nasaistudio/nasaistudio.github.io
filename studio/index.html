FAQ

Questions brands ask first.

What kinds of products do you work with?

Almost anything you sell: clothing and shoes, beauty and skincare, fragrance, food and drinks, tech and gadgets, jewellery and watches, bags, home goods, and apps or services too.

What do I need to send?

A clear photo of the product, even from a phone. Add any notes on the look you want, the kind of model, the script or idea, and where the content will be used.

Will the product look like mine?

Yes. The shape, colours, label and details are kept true to the original, and you review everything. If a detail is off, ask for a change before you take the final files.

Are the models real people?

No. Every model and presenter is fictional and AI-generated. Real faces are not used as references.

How long does it take?

Usually 1 to 3 days, depending on how many pieces you need.

How does the free sample work?

Send one product photo and I will make a sample from it, so you can judge the quality before you order anything.

Do the ads say they are AI?

Yes. Video ads carry an "AI-generated creator" label, and presenters show and explain the product without pretending to be real customers. See the Responsible AI policy.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of products and the pieces you need for each. Send your product photos and I will quote within a day.
