06 · FAQ

Questions.

What do I need to send?

A clear photo of the garment: a flat-lay, a hanger shot or a phone photo. Add any notes on the look you want, the kind of model and where the images will be used.

Will the garment look like mine?

Yes. The garment is kept true to the original, and you review every image. If a detail is off, ask for a change before you take the final files.

Are the models real people?

No. Every model and presenter is fictional and AI-generated. Real faces are not used as references.

How long does it take?

Usually 1 to 3 days, depending on how many pieces you need.

How does the free sample work?

Send one product photo and I will make a sample from it, so you can judge the quality before you order anything.

Do the ads say they are AI?

Yes. Video ads carry an "AI-generated creator" label, and presenters show and explain the product without pretending to be real customers. See the Responsible AI policy.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of garments and the pieces per garment. Send your product photos and I will quote within a day.
