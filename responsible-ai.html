Responsible AI

How NAS AI Studio handles consent, voices, people and disclosure. This applies to every project.

Voices

  • Your own voice only with consent. I make an AI version of a real person's voice only when that person gives written consent, and only for their own content.
  • No celebrity or public-figure voices, and no voices you don't have the rights to.
  • Stock AI voices are used whenever no consent is given.
  • My own samples open with my real, recorded consent.

Content

  • You must own the content you send, or have the right to translate and adapt it.
  • No impersonation, no deepfakes, no misleading, political or harmful content.
  • All AI models and presenters shown are fictional people.

Ads and presenters

  • UGC-style ads use AI presenters who show and explain a product. They do not pretend to be real customers or give fake reviews.
  • I recommend labelling AI content where the platform asks for it, as Meta and TikTok do.

Disclosure

  • Everything on this site is AI-generated unless it says otherwise.
  • Translations are reviewed line by line, and names and terms you give me are kept exactly.

Questions

If you're unsure whether a project fits these rules, message me before ordering and I will tell you honestly.
